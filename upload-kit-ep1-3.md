# Munchlings: Upload Kit for Episodes 1–3 (copy and paste)

Everything you type into YouTube Studio when you upload, in **German (main)** and **English (Studio → Languages)**. It also has the Shorts, the parent Reels and a launch calendar.

**Settings for every episode**
- Audience: **Yes, it's made for kids**
- Category: **Education** · Video language: **German**
- Altered or synthetic content: YouTube only requires this for AI content that **looks real**, so a cartoon doesn't need it. I'd still tick **Yes**: it's honest, it builds trust with parents, and the descriptions already say the animation is AI-made.
- Playlist: **Munchlings – Alle Folgen**. In English: *Munchlings – All Episodes*.
- Audio: upload the English track in **Languages → Add audio**. Then turn on **auto-dubbing** for the other languages.

**Website links:** the recipe pages are already in `website/rezepte/` and `website/en/recipes/`. They only work after you upload the website.
