Mini Pizza Toasts

From Episode 2: Tim says sorry, and then everyone makes something yummy together. The fastest pizza snack ever!

  • 🥕 3 ingredients
  • ⏱️ Prep: 5 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 8 min
  • 🍽️ Makes: 8 mini toasts

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • ½ cup grated cheese (halal-certified if you like)

How to make it

  1. 👩 Grown-up Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. 👩 Grown-up Cut each slice of bread into 4 small squares and put them on a baking tray.
  3. Spread a little tomato sauce on each square.
  4. Sprinkle the cheese on top.
  5. 👩 Grown-up Bake for 6–8 minutes, until the cheese melts.
  6. 👩 Grown-up Let them cool for 2 minutes: melted cheese is very hot!

⚠️ Always cook with a grown-up. Melted cheese stays hot for a long time. Check it before serving.

Contains: gluten (bread), dairy (cheese)
