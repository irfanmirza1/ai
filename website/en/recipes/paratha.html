Simple Paratha

From Episode 3: the Magic Lunchbox takes the Munchlings to Pakistan. Paratha is a round, flaky flatbread that many people there love for breakfast. Mazedaar!

  • 🥕 3 ingredients
  • ⏱️ Prep: 25 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 10 min
  • 🍽️ Makes: 3 parathas

Ingredients

  • 1 cup wheat flour (atta) with a pinch of salt
  • about ⅓ cup water
  • ghee or butter

How to make it

  1. Put the flour and salt in a bowl. Add the water a little at a time and knead it into a soft dough.
  2. Cover the dough and let it rest for 15 minutes. Then split it into 3 balls.
  3. Roll one ball flat and spread a thin layer of ghee on it.
  4. Fold the dough in half, fold it again, then roll it flat again. That makes the layers!
  5. 👩 Grown-up Heat a pan (or tawa) on medium. Cook the paratha for 1–2 minutes on each side with a little ghee, until it has golden spots.
  6. 👩 Grown-up Let it cool a little, then tear it into pieces and enjoy.

⚠️ Always cook with a grown-up. The pan gets very hot: only a grown-up does the cooking.

Contains: gluten (flour), dairy (ghee/butter)

Episode 3: Food Passport: PAKISTAN!

🧠 The fact

Paratha is flaky because the dough is folded into layers.

🔤 The word

Mazedaar! (Urdu for “delicious”)
